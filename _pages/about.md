I am a tenure-track Associate Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at the
[School of Biomedical Engineering](https://bme.sjtu.edu.cn/),
Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I joined Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2023.

My research focuses on **neural function reconstruction**, **intelligent
human–machine interaction**, **brain–computer interfaces**, and
**embodied intelligence**. Motivated by the rehabilitation needs of individuals
with paralysis and motor impairments, my group develops integrated systems that
connect human neural and motor information sensing, intention decoding, and
assistive or substitutive execution.

Our research combines biomedical engineering, electronic information,
artificial intelligence, robotics, medical chips, and intelligent hardware.
We are particularly interested in translating these technologies into
implantable artificial spinal cords, artificial neural systems, neural
prostheses, exoskeletons, rehabilitation robots, and embodied robotic systems.

Over the past three years, I have published more than 50 papers and served as
principal investigator on two projects funded by the National Natural Science
Foundation of China and two provincial or ministerial-level projects. I have
also been selected for the Shanghai Overseas High-Level Talent Program and the
Shanghai Pujiang Talent Program.

Beyond academic research, I place strong emphasis on interdisciplinary
collaboration and technology translation. Several of our research outcomes
have been commercialized as prosthetic products. I have also collaborated with
Huawei Central Research Institute on human–machine interaction technologies
for wearable devices. Before joining academia, I worked at the Shanghai
Municipal Development and Reform Commission, where I contributed to policy
reform and strategic planning for science and technology industries.

## Research Philosophy

Our group follows a full-stack research pathway:

**Human sensing → intention decoding → intelligent control → assistive execution**

We develop electronic circuits and sensing systems, machine-learning
algorithms, embedded software, host-computer software, and robotic platforms.
Our work emphasizes hardware–software co-design, clinical validation, and
real-world translation.
