I am a tenure-track Associate Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at the
[School of Biomedical Engineering](https://bme.sjtu.edu.cn/),
Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research focuses on **neural function reconstruction**, **intelligent
human–machine interaction**, and **brain–computer interfaces with embodied
intelligence**. We develop integrated systems spanning neural and motor
information sensing, intention decoding, intelligent control, and assistive
execution.

Our work integrates biomedical engineering, artificial intelligence,
robotics, medical electronics, and intelligent hardware, with applications
in neural prostheses, artificial spinal cords, exoskeletons, rehabilitation
systems, and embodied robots.
