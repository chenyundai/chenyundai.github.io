I am a tenure-track Associate Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at the
[School of Biomedical Engineering](https://bme.sjtu.edu.cn/En/),
[Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/). I joined the faculty
in 2023.

My research seeks to **restore and augment human motor function** by combining
medical electronics, neural and motor biosensing, artificial intelligence,
and robotics. We develop complete systems that connect physiological signal
acquisition, human-intent decoding, intelligent control, and assistive or
stimulation-based execution.

Our current programs span implantable artificial spinal cords and neural
systems for people with paralysis, intelligent human–machine interaction for
neural prostheses and exoskeletons, and brain–computer interfaces for embodied
robots that learn human movement from motion capture, inertial sensing, and
surface electromyography.

## Translation and impact

Our group works at the interface of discovery, engineering, and deployment.
Multiple prosthetic technologies have been translated into commercial
products and featured in a follow-up report by a Boston television station.
Our human–machine
interaction research has also supported the development of an interaction
module for Huawei Watch through funding from Huawei Central Research
Institute.

Over the past three years, I have published more than 50 papers and led two
National Natural Science Foundation of China projects and two projects at the
provincial or ministerial level. I have been recognized by the
Shanghai Overseas High-Level Talent Program and the Shanghai Pujiang Talent
Program.

Before joining Shanghai Jiao Tong University, I worked at the Shanghai
Municipal Development and Reform Commission, where I advanced policy reforms
for industry–academia–research collaboration and contributed the chapter on
emerging technology industries to Shanghai's 14th Five-Year Plan.
